# Answer an open question

Use this mode for “what does this do?”, comparisons, landscape questions, or questions about how a
practice works.

## Frame the actual question

Restate the requested outcome in concrete terms. Decompose broad requests into independently verifiable
subquestions while preserving named entities, comparisons, dates, measures, and output constraints.
Distinguish a focused lookup from a broad synthesis; do not turn a direct question into an unnecessary
survey.

## Gather evidence by claim

Search for original artifacts and claim-specific authority:

- official material for release facts and contracts;
- source, tests, and reproducible demonstrations for mechanisms;
- appropriately matched evaluations for comparative performance;
- firsthand users for local workflows and failure reports;
- independent sources and counterevidence for generalization.

Keep a small evidence inventory outside the prose: claim, state, source coordinate or span, claimant,
authority for this claim, shared lineage, contradiction, freshness, and important limitation. Distinct
URLs or domains are not automatically independent evidence.

For broad questions, check coverage before stopping: are the material subquestions addressed, are major
perspectives or counterexamples missing, and would another search plausibly change the answer? Use these
gaps rather than a universal source quota.

## Synthesize once the evidence is ready

Write the answer from retained claims instead of repeatedly rewriting a running summary. Clearly
separate:

- what was directly observed;
- what sources claim;
- what follows by analysis;
- what remains unknown or contested.

Cite material sourced claims near the text they support. A citation must point to evidence that actually
supports the claim, not merely a page the process visited.

If the user supplied local context, finish with implications for that context and use the artifact-mode
verdicts where appropriate. Without local context, explain what facts or constraints would determine
adoption rather than inventing a recommendation.
